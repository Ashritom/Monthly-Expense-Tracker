# Monthly-Expense-Tracker

## Monthly Expense & Financial Planning Tracker — Excel

A reusable and downloadable **Microsoft Excel template** designed to help individuals plan their monthly income, prioritize important financial commitments, and systematically track their day-to-day expenses.

Instead of focusing only on recording expenses after they occur, this tracker follows a **plan-first approach**: users first decide how their monthly income should be allocated toward essential financial priorities and then manage their remaining amount through expense tracking.

### Key Features

* **Monthly Income Planning**

  * Enter the total monthly income available for the month.
  * Use percentage-based allocation to determine how much of the income should be reserved for important financial priorities.

* **Priority-Based Income Allocation**

  * Allocate a chosen percentage of income toward:

    * Home Contribution
    * Insurance
    * Investments
    * Emergency Fund
  * The allocated amounts are calculated automatically based on the income and percentages entered.
  * The remaining income is automatically identified as the amount available for regular expenses.

* **Expense Tracking**

  * Record individual expenses throughout the month.
  * Each expense entry includes:

    * Date
    * Item/Description
    * Expense Category
    * Amount
  * This allows users to maintain a structured record of where their money is being spent.

* **Automated Financial Calculations**

  * Automatically calculates the amounts allocated to each financial priority.
  * Calculates the total amount spent on expenses.
  * Tracks the remaining amount available after expenses.
  * Updates the financial overview as new expense entries are added.

* **Data Validation**

  * Uses Excel's data validation features to make expense entries more consistent and reduce incorrect or inconsistent category inputs.

* **Clean and Minimal Layout**

  * Designed with a simple, structured interface rather than relying on Excel's default table styling.
  * Uses formatting and borders to separate sections and improve readability while keeping the workbook visually clean.

* **Reusable Monthly Template**

  * Provided as a downloadable `.xlsx` file.
  * Can be reused for different months and customized according to an individual's income, financial priorities, and spending categories.

### Purpose

The primary purpose of this project is to make **monthly financial planning more structured and easier to understand**.

By separating income allocation from day-to-day expenses, the tracker encourages users to consider important financial commitments such as **investments and emergency savings before determining how much is available for discretionary spending**.

The resulting overview helps users understand:

* How their income is being allocated
* How much is reserved for financial priorities
* How much is available for regular expenses
* Where their money is being spent
* How much they have remaining after expenses
